# Phase 2b: Fix the linker recall gap (blocks Phase 3)

Reviewer finding on Phase 2: the report says every sample case links purely through direct identity keys and that the 60% recall is entirely coverage-based absence (Phase 3's job). That is only half true. Of the 44 expected (system_id, resource_id) pairs not linked, 24 are not in any visible observation (coverage-based, fine), but 20 ARE in visible observations and were left unlinked.

Measured by the reviewer:
- Log lines are grouped by `trace_id`. The person's identifier (`lookup_key`, `subject_ref`, `correlation_key`) is on one line of a trace and the `resource_id` on another. The log parser does not carry identity across a trace. Linking by trace recovers 6 of the 20 (for example a not-found account lookup whose start line holds the email).
- The other 14 (analytics reports with null identity, several account/analytics pairs in cases 21 and 25-28, a backup and a data-flow record in cases 15 and 17) have no identifier on the observation itself. How they tie to a person is not yet established.
- case_17 has an extra linked account the sample does not list while an expected account is unlinked. That may be a false link.
- The activity stream has no user column. A time window of requested_at..reported_at does NOT work as a key (windows hold 15-70 decoy rows, and only 44 of 111 expected resources fall inside).
